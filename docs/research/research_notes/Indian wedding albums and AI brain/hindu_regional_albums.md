# Hindu wedding albums across India's regions: functions, decisive frames, and how photos are selected

Method note (read first): in this session only the web search tool worked. Every page fetch failed
(the cloud machine's network blocks slrlounge.com, wedmegood.com, weddingwire.in, zola.com, canvera.com,
en.wikipedia.org and others). So every finding below comes from search-result summaries with quoted
snippets, not from full pages I read end to end. Each finding keeps the URL the summary came from. Most
sources are photographers' marketing blogs and wedding-vendor guides: useful for current practice,
weak as authority. Where they disagree, that is noted.

## 1. North Indian / Punjabi Hindu, Rajasthani, Bihari/Maithil, Garhwali/Kumaoni: functions and must-have shots

### Takeaway
Across the north the album runs: pre-wedding (roka/tilak/sagai, haldi or pithi, mehendi, sangeet) ->
baraat (groom on mare, sehra) -> family meeting at the door (milni / toran / dwar puja) -> jaimala ->
kanyadaan -> pheras -> sindoor + mangalsutra -> games (joota chupai) -> vidaai -> griha pravesh ->
reception. The decisive frames photographers single out are the garland exchange, the sindoor moment,
the pheras round the fire and the vidaai (the "no second take" frame). Regional twists: Rajasthani toran
strike and sword on the mare; Maithil sindoor under a held cloth (private) and the painted kohbar room;
Kumaoni fathers exchanging rings and group photos straight after the garlands.

### Cited Findings
**Pan-North Indian / Punjabi**
- Common must-have list across Indian wedding guides: getting-ready shots of bride and groom dressing, a clean photo of the bridal outfit, close-ups of jewellery "with a soft light"; the varmala/jaimala garland exchange "amidst cheers and laughter"; the sindoor moment; family portraits planned in advance, "start with the largest groups before working down to smaller combinations"; reception speeches with the couple's reactions; candid dance-floor shots; and "a well-shot image of the lavish buffet, desserts, or even guests enjoying the delicacies" — [WeddingBazaar checklist](https://www.weddingbazaar.com/blog/wedding-moments-photo-checklist); [VenueLook 15 must-have shots](https://www.venuelook.com/blog/15-must-have-shots-in-your-wedding-album); [BollywoodShaadis, bride's must-haves](https://www.bollywoodshaadis.com/articles/top-10-photos-every-indian-bride-must-have-in-her-wedding-album-3531)
- Groom-side gap: "the fun moments of mehendi usually get ignored" for the groom; guides say his haldi/mehendi deserve album space — [BollywoodShaadis, groom's must-haves](https://www.bollywoodshaadis.com/articles/top-10-photos-every-indian-groom-must-have-in-his-wedding-album-3592)
- Punjabi day-of order: Milni, Varmala, Joota chupai, then the post-wedding Vidaai — [Shan Photography, 14 must-capture Punjabi rituals](https://www.shanphotography.com/blog/14-endearing-must-capture-punjabi-wedding-rituals/); [Wikipedia, Punjabi wedding traditions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjabi_wedding_traditions)
- Milni: the baraat arrives and is met at the entrance by the bride's party; relatives are paired by role (e.g. groom's maternal uncles with bride's maternal uncles); photographers are told to capture "the joy and warmth" — [Fine Art Production, Punjabi wedding photography](https://www.fineartproduction.com/post/punjabi-wedding-traditions-punjabi-wedding-photography); [Shan Photography](https://www.shanphotography.com/blog/14-endearing-must-capture-punjabi-wedding-rituals/)
- Joota chupai: the bride's sisters offer milk and hide the groom's shoes for ransom; "an entertaining round of playfulness and haggling"; typically in the evening — [Fine Art Production](https://www.fineartproduction.com/post/punjabi-wedding-traditions-punjabi-wedding-photography); [Manoj Lohar essential shot list](https://manojlohar.com/essential-shot-list-indian-wedding-photography/)
- Vidaai: called "the frame no one gets a second chance at"; plan for the bride throwing rice back over her head without looking back, and the family's emotional goodbyes — [Manoj Lohar](https://manojlohar.com/essential-shot-list-indian-wedding-photography/); [Shan Photography](https://www.shanphotography.com/blog/14-endearing-must-capture-punjabi-wedding-rituals/)
- Photographers advise couples to give "a list of your must-include shots", but "remain flexible"; a Hindu-ceremony guide warns not to assume a family's practice since traditions cross communities — [Manoj Lohar](https://manojlohar.com/essential-shot-list-indian-wedding-photography/); [SLR Lounge Hindu ceremony guide](https://www.slrlounge.com/indian-wedding-photography-guide-for-hindu-wedding-ceremony/) (summary only)

**Rajasthani / Marwari**
- One ordered list: Tika/Sagai, Byah Haath, Gangaur Puja and Naandi Ganesh Puja, Bhaat Nyotana, Mahira Dastoor, Pithi Dastoor/Haldi, Mehndi, Sangeet/Mehfil, Tel Baan, Nikasi and Baraat, Toran, then mandap rites — [Rajasthani Marwari ceremonies in order (laddothis guide)](https://coderalert0.github.io/laddothis-site/guides/rajasthani-marwari-wedding-ceremonies/)
- Another core sequence: Tilak, Ganpati Sthapana, Pithi Dastoor, Mahira Dastoor, Toran, Jaimala, Pheras, Saptapadi, Sindoor Daan, Kanyadaan, Vidaai, Grihapravesh (note: puts kanyadaan after sindoor, unlike most north Indian lists) — [Velvet Knot, Marwari traditions](https://velvetknot.in/marwari-wedding-traditions/); [WeddingSutra, Marwari weddings](https://weddingsutra.com/planning/wedding-tradition-series-soak-up-the-royal-traditions-of-marwari-weddings/)
- Tilak timing disagrees: one source says it happens long before, even before engagement; another says the bride's brother applies tilak to the groom at the engagement at the groom's home — [BollywoodShaadis, Marwari rituals](https://www.bollywoodshaadis.com/articles/marwari-wedding-traditional-rituals-journey-to-happily-ever-after-from-tilak-to-pagphera-37044); [CulturalIndia, Marwari wedding](https://www.culturalindia.net/weddings/regional-weddings/marwari-wedding.html)
- Nikasi: groom's sister performs it before departure; sehra tied, kajal applied, golden threads on the mare's reins; groom rides a mare carrying a sword — [Aza Fashions, Rajasthan traditions](https://www.azafashions.com/blog/rajasthan-wedding-traditions-rituals-cultural-guide-2026/); [KnotsbyAMP, Marwari weddings](https://www.knotsbyamp.com/blog/rich-traditions-of-royal-marwari-weddingsnbsp)
- Toran: the groom strikes the decorated arch at the venue entrance with a neem stick (wards off the evil eye); the bride's mother receives him with aarti and sweets — [City of Jaipur, Marwari rituals](https://www.cityofjaipur.com/lets-talk-jaipur/wedding-rituals-you-ll-see-in-a-typical-marwari-wedding-in-rajasthan/); [Rajasthan Studio, 7 rituals](https://rajasthanstudio.com/do-you-know-7-vibrant-marriage-rituals-of-rajasthan/)
- Ghoomar (women's circular dance) appears at pithi/haldi gatherings, and the new bride is expected to dance ghoomar when welcomed into her marital home — [Wikipedia, Ghoomar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghoomar)

**Bihari / Maithil**
- Common sequence: Chekni and Siddhori, Tilak, Matkor, Haldi with Imli Ghotai, baraat with Dwar Puja and Parichhawan, Jaimala, Kanyadaan, Bhaanwar and Saptapadi, Sindoor Daan, Kohbar and Chaturthi rites, Bidaai — [Velvet Knot, Bihari traditions](https://velvetknot.in/bihari-wedding-traditions/)
- Another source puts Parichhan first, then the groom is taken to the Kohbar room, where Naina-Jogin happens first — [Ratopati, Maithil marriage](https://english.ratopati.com/story/46637/maithil-marriage-a-streamlined-system)
- Kohbar ghar: room in the bride's house where the family deity is installed and rites take place; walls painted with lotus ponds, bamboo, fish (fertility, union) — a distinctive "detail" shot — [Wikipedia, Maithil Vivah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maithil_Vivah); [Velvet Knot](https://velvetknot.in/bihari-wedding-traditions/)
- Sindoor daan method disagrees: groom applies vermilion five times with a mango leaf, coin or ring while a cloth is held over the couple, kept private ([MithilaOnline](https://mithilaonline.com/mithila-rituals/)); groom uses a ring, not his hand ([KnowBihar](https://knowbihar.in/en/post/bihari-wedding-rituals)); applied to forehead and parting five times round the fire during pheras ([Shaadhi Weddings](https://shaadhiweddings.com/bihari-wedding-rituals/))
- Maithil brides traditionally get pink "Bhuswa" sindoor, not red (relevant to colour-based sindoor detection) — [Mithila lifestyle and culture blog](https://mithilalifestyleandculture.design.blog/2020/09/06/marriage-in-mithila-maithil-vivah/)

**Garhwali / Kumaoni (Uttarakhand)**
- Kumaoni sequence: matchmaking/horoscopes, engagement, women's suwal-making, Ganesh puja, haldi, baraat, groom's welcome at dusk, then kanyadaan and pheras — [Velvet Knot, Kumaoni and Garhwali](https://velvetknot.in/kumaoni-garhwali-wedding-traditions/)
- Kumaoni engagement: "the fathers of the to be wedded couple exchange the rings instead of the bride and groom" — [BYOH, Kumaoni rituals](https://blog.byoh.in/traditional-kumaoni-wedding-rituals)
- Kumaoni photo timing: right after the garland exchange "the camera starts rolling to take out wonderful pictures of the couple along with their family, friends, and relatives", then the feast — [Uttarakhandshadi, Kumaoni rituals](https://uttarakhandshadi.com/blog/the-fascinating-wedding-rituals-of-the-kumaoni-people)
- Garhwali pre-wedding: Arsa making, Keertan, Baand Snaan or Haldi Haath, Bhiksha, Dal Pakodi making, Mehandi night, Sehrah bandhayi, Shehrah bhenth; post-wedding Dan, Bidai, Reception; can run up to five days — [The Wedding Focus, Garhwali rituals](https://www.theweddingfocus.com/blog/garhwali-wedding-rituals/); [BYOH, Garhwali](https://blog.byoh.in/traditional-garhwali-wedding-ideas)
- Late-night Kumaoni rituals compared to Rajasthani weddings (passing remark only) — [Travel Shoebum, Kumaoni wedding](https://travelshoebum.com/2017/09/05/attending-a-kumaoni-wedding-in-kumaon/)

### Inferences
- For the app: the north's decisive frames are fast physical actions (garland over the head, sindoor at the parting, rice thrown at vidaai, shoe ransom) that occur in dense bursts. This supports the existing "big moments as dense shooting bursts" model: the peak frame is the one where the hand/garland is in contact and both faces are visible, not the frames before/after.
- The pheras are long and repetitive (many near-identical frames round the fire): collapse to a few, ideally one with the fire visible and both faces.
- Milni and toran are family-heavy door moments with many elder men; they feed "both sides' elders" coverage.
- Maithil sindoor under a cloth may produce few or occluded frames: an occlusion penalty should not knock it out entirely.

### Gaps
- No full shot list was readable for roka, griha pravesh or the mangalsutra moment in north Indian weddings beyond summaries; Rajasthani and Bihari sources gave ritual order but no photography guidance.
- No source found on Garhwali/Kumaoni album conventions specifically.

## 2. Bengali Hindu, Odia, Assamese Hindu

### Takeaway
Bengali albums revolve around shubho drishti (the first look when the betel leaves come down) — called the single most important photo of a Bengali wedding — plus sindoor daan (applied with a ring/coin/bamboo cup), saat paak, mala badal, gaye holud and bou bhaat. Assamese sequence (juron -> tel diya -> nuoni -> biya) is documented; Odia (bahaghara) is thinly covered.

### Cited Findings
- Bengali order: aiburobhat, gaye holud, shubho drishti, mala badal, saat paak, sindoor daan, bou bhaat (bashor ghor usually after sindoor daan) — [Camrin Films, Kolkata](https://camrinfilms.com/wedding-photographer-kolkata); [Birdlens, Bengali rituals](https://www.birdlenscreation.com/blog/bengali-wedding-rituals)
- Shubho drishti: treated as the centrepiece; one studio calls it the single most important photograph in a Bengali wedding; bride hides her face behind betel leaves and the reveal is the frame; a Siliguri studio arrives ~30 minutes early to secure a clear sightline to both faces; a Kolkata studio often shoots it in slow motion — [Jeet Biswas Photography, Bengali checklist](https://jeetbiswasphotography.com/?p=19805); [Suvo Weddings](https://suvoweddings.netlify.app/blog-post-3.html); [Kolkata Wedding Photographers, top 10](https://kolkataweddingphotographers.com/top-10-must-have-shots-in-a-bengali-wedding-photography-album/)
- Sindoor daan: "a customary Bengali photo that every Bengali wedding album contains"; groom applies with a ring, coin or small bamboo cup — the tool itself is a close-up; common framing is a tight shallow-focus frame of the application with faces soft behind — [Clickstory, Bengali photography](https://clickstory.in/?p=32269); [Royal Bindi, Bengali photography](https://royalbindi.co.uk/bengali-wedding-photography)
- Saat paak: bride carried round the groom seven times; shoot wide for the scene then close for expressions — [Royal Bindi, Bengali traditions](https://royalbindi.co.uk/blog/bengali-wedding-traditions)
- Mala badal: fast and chaotic, "burst mode" recommended — [Suvo Weddings](https://suvoweddings.netlify.app/blog-post-3.html)
- Gaye holud: late-afternoon outdoor light recommended — [Jeet Biswas](https://jeetbiswasphotography.com/?p=19805)
- Bashor ghor: bride's sister stops the couple at the door and asks the groom for money to enter (playful candid) — [Birdlens](https://www.birdlenscreation.com/blog/bengali-wedding-rituals)
- Bou bhaat: post-wedding feast hosted by the groom's family for both families a day or two after; on its morning the groom gives the bride a plate of food and a new saree; studios list "Reception & Bou Bhat" as its own album section — [Wikipedia, Bou Bhat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bou_Bhat); [Birdlens](https://www.birdlenscreation.com/blog/bengali-wedding-rituals); [Biswas Photography](https://biswasphotography.in/bengali-wedding-photography/)
- Bengali coverage also expected: bride's getting-ready sequence, family/group portraits, a pre-wedding shoot in traditional attire, drone shots for outdoor or large events — [Kolkata Wedding Photographers](https://kolkataweddingphotographers.com/top-10-must-have-shots-in-a-bengali-wedding-photography-album/); [Aza Fashions, Bengali style](https://www.azafashions.com/blog/bengali-wedding-style-guide/)
- Assamese: Juron (groom's mother brings the trousseau, 1-2 days before); Tel Diya within juron (mother-in-law puts a betel nut on the bride's head, pours oil three times); Nuoni ceremonial baths (likened to haldi + mangal snanam); Biya before the fire, groom puts sindoor in the parting, 7 pheras — [Assamese ceremonies in order (laddothis)](https://coderalert0.github.io/laddothis-site/guides/assamese-hindu-wedding-ceremonies/); [Utsavpedia, Assam wedding](https://www.utsavpedia.com/weddings-festivals/wedding-in-assam/); [CulturalIndia, Assamese wedding](https://www.culturalindia.net/weddings/regional-weddings/assamese-wedding.html)
- Assamese sindoor disagreement: one source says the groom applies it at the wedding, another that the mother-in-law applies it at juron — [Pernia's Pop-Up, Assam](https://www.perniaspopupshop.com/wedding-encyclopedia/assam); [Vishwaratna Hotel](https://vishwaratnahotel.com/assamese-wedding-traditions/)
- Odia: the Hindu wedding is "Bahaghara"; engagement called nirbandha or lagna dharaa; "Jwaiñ anukuḷa" starts the rites at the groom's house — [Wikipedia, Odia Hindu wedding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odia_Hindu_wedding)

### Inferences
- Shubho drishti is a "reveal" moment: the best frame has the bride's face uncovered and eyes open looking at the groom; frames with leaves still over the face are the build-up. A "looking at each other" bonus fits it exactly; a "face blocked" penalty must not apply to the build-up frames chosen as a sequence.
- Bengali weddings are often in the evening/night under artificial light; the app's per-event lighting judgement matters.

### Gaps
- Nothing found on kanakanjali (bride's farewell offering of rice to her mother) or aiburobhat photography, nor on Odia (e.g. hatagranthi, saptapadi, kanyadaan) or Assamese album conventions.

## 3. Marathi and Gujarati

### Takeaway
Marathi decisive frame: the antarpat (cloth screen) coming down after the mangalashtak, followed straight away by the garland exchange; relatives singing the mangalashtak are good candids. Gujarati albums cover pithi, mameru (maternal uncle's gifts), mandap mahurat, the jaan, hast melap, four mangal pheras and kanya viday tears.

### Cited Findings
- Antarpat: a white cloth, often with a swastik, held between bride and groom so they cannot see each other; "As the Antarpat is removed, the couple sees each other for the first time on their wedding day" — [WeddingWire India, Mangalashtak](https://www.weddingwire.in/wedding-tips/mangalashtak-in-marriage--c4807); [VenueLook, Marathi traditions](https://www.venuelook.com/blog/marathi-wedding-traditions-all-you-need-to-know-about-their-beautiful-rituals)
- Order disagreement: cloth removed after the priest finishes the mantras ([WeddingWire India, Maharashtrian guide](https://www.weddingwire.in/wedding-tips/maharashtrian-wedding--c754)) versus "when the wedding vows are exchanged" ([BollywoodShaadis, Maharashtrian](https://mirror2stevejobs.bollywoodshaadis.com/articles/sacred-rituals-of-a-maharashtrian-wedding-1861)); garlands are exchanged right after the antarpat ([VenueLook](https://www.venuelook.com/blog/marathi-wedding-traditions-all-you-need-to-know-about-their-beautiful-rituals))
- Mangalashtak: priest recites eight verses; relatives of both sides sing along and bless the couple — [WeddingWire India, Mangalashtak](https://www.weddingwire.in/wedding-tips/mangalashtak-in-marriage--c4807)
- The bride presents the groom a bouquet and touches his feet for blessings (elder/respect frame) — [WeddingWire India, Maharashtrian](https://www.weddingwire.in/wedding-tips/maharashtrian-wedding--c754)
- Gujarati pithi: paste mixed traditionally by the kaaki (paternal uncle's wife) — sandalwood, herbs, rosewater, mogra/attar; another source lists sandalwood, turmeric, saffron, rosewater, perfume — [Kaushik Bathia, Gujarati traditions](https://www.kaushikbathiaphotography.co.uk/blog-gujarati-wedding-traditions-photography); [Alfaaz Photography](https://www.alfaazphotography.com/5-things-you-need-to-know-before-photographing-gujarati-weddings/)
- Mameru (mosaalu): 1-2 days before; the bride's maternal uncle brings sweets and gifts (sarees, jewellery, clothes) — [Kaushik Bathia](https://www.kaushikbathiaphotography.co.uk/blog-gujarati-wedding-traditions-photography)
- Key Gujarati moments: jaan (baraat), hast melap (joining hands), four mangal pheras with the bride leading the first — [Monterey Films, Gujarati](https://montereyfilms.com/weddings/gujarati-weddings); [Raxprit, Gujarati](https://raxprit.co.uk/gujarati-wedding-photography/)
- Kanya viday: emotional farewell, "tears of joy during Vidai" part of candid coverage — [Magical Click](https://magicalclick.in/gujarati-wedding-photographer-near-me/)
- Studio coverage lists Pithi, Mehndi, Garba/Sangeet night, Mameru and Mandap Mahurat; Gujarati photography "also demands a mastery of portraiture and traditional poses" (posed family portraits valued alongside candids) — [Uthsaav, Gujarati](https://uthsaav.com/gujarati-wedding); [Alfaaz](https://www.alfaazphotography.com/5-things-you-need-to-know-before-photographing-gujarati-weddings/)

### Inferences
- Antarpat is structurally like Bengali shubho drishti: a screen then a reveal, so the same "reveal peak" logic applies.
- Mameru is a maternal-uncle-centric function: the app's people coverage should not starve the mama's family.
- Garba/sangeet nights are under coloured light with heavy motion: judge against their own event.

### Gaps
- No usable source on sakharpuda, halad (Marathi haldi), simant puja, saptapadi or vidaai/pathavani photography; nor on Gujarati mandap mahurat shots.

## 4. South Indian: Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayali/Kerala Hindu

### Takeaway
South Indian ceremonies are morning muhurtham ceremonies where the thali/mangalsutra tying at the exact minute is the decisive frame. Tamil adds kashi yatra and oonjal (fast, unrepeated, easily missed); Telugu adds jeelakarra bellam (cumin-jaggery paste on each other's heads, the muhurtham itself), talambralu (playful rice/pearl shower) and Arundhati star-gazing; Kannada's centre is dhaare (water poured over joined hands); Kerala's core rituals take only 60-90 minutes and are timed to the minute.

### Cited Findings
- Tamil shot list: kashi yatra, maalai maatral, oonjal, mangalya dharanam (thali tying), saptapadi, candid reactions, reception portraits, vidaai; also nischayathartham and pre-wedding blessings — [KnotStories, traditional Tamil photography](https://knotstories.in/traditional-tamil-wedding-photography-rituals-you-shouldnt-miss/); [HiQ Weddings, Tamil Brahmin guide](https://www.hiqweddings.net/blog/tamil-brahmin-wedding-photography-guide)
- Oonjal and kashi yatra are fast and seldom repeated; photographers unfamiliar with the sequence risk missing them; not every photographer knows oonjal from nalangu — [HiQ Weddings](https://www.hiqweddings.net/blog/tamil-brahmin-wedding-photography-guide); [ClickFactory, Brahmin rituals](https://clickfactoryphotography.com/rituals-to-capture-in-brahmin-wedding-photography/)
- Kashi yatra: focus on "candid expressions and humor"; maalai maatral: friends trying to block the view / lift the couple; oonjal: candids plus tears and blessings — [iGlow Studioz, Tamil Brahmin](https://iglowstudioz.com/2025/04/24/tamil-brahmin-wedding-photography-ideas-to-capture-every-sacred-moment/)
- Nalangu: "cheerful banter" with sentimental value, but no shot guidance found — [KnotStories](https://knotstories.in/traditional-tamil-wedding-photography-rituals-you-shouldnt-miss/)
- Tamil Brahmin ritual order and detail differ from other Tamil traditions — [HiQ Weddings](https://www.hiqweddings.net/blog/tamil-brahmin-wedding-photography-guide)
- Historic reference: British Library's archive of Tamil studio photography includes a frame of the groom tying the talikayiru on the bride's neck — [British Library EAP946](https://eap.bl.uk/index.php/project/EAP946/search?page=165)
- Telugu pellikuthuru: 1-2 days before at the bride's place, turmeric-flour paste then holy bath; emotional frames cited: "Mothers with tears in their eyes, sisters laughing as they apply haldi", grandmothers blessing — [Telugu ceremonies in order (laddothis)](https://coderalert0.github.io/laddothis-site/guides/telugu-hindu-wedding-ceremonies/); [Digiart Photography, Hyderabad](https://digiartphotography.com/telugu-wedding-photography-hyderabad-2/)
- Jeelakarra bellam: paste of cumin and jaggery on a betel leaf placed on each other's heads while the purohit chants — [The News Minute, Kajal Aggarwal wedding](https://www.thenewsminute.com/amp/story/flix/jeelakarra-bellam-telugu-connection-kajal-aggarwal-s-wedding-ceremony-136631); [Third Eye Studio, Telugu wedding Dallas](https://thirdeyestudio.mypixieset.com/blog/Telugu-Wedding-in-Dallas/)
- Telugu order as a photographer positions for it: "Jeelakarra bellam at the muhurtham, then mangalsutra dharana, then talambralu" — [1Plus1 Studio, Telugu guide](https://www.1plus1studio.com/telugu-wedding-guide)
- Talambralu: "the most playful moment"; couple shower each other with pearls, thermocol balls or turmeric rice; it and the garland exchange "happen quickly and rarely pause for a second take" — [HiQ Weddings, Telugu](https://www.hiqweddings.net/blog/telugu-wedding-photography-and-rituals); [1Plus1 Studio](https://www.1plus1studio.com/telugu-wedding-guide)
- Arundhati nakshatram: the priest takes the couple out of the mandapam to spot the Arundhati and Vasishtha stars (often dusk/night outdoors) — [laddothis Telugu guide](https://coderalert0.github.io/laddothis-site/guides/telugu-hindu-wedding-ceremonies/)
- Telugu ceremonies often happen at dawn under function-hall lighting — [1Plus1 Studio](https://www.1plus1studio.com/telugu-wedding-guide)
- Kannada order: Nischaya Tamboola, Naandi, turmeric bath; then Kashi Yatra, Var Puja, garland exchange, Dhaare, Mangalya Dharane, Saptapadi; then games like Okhli and griha pravesha — [Velvet Knot, Kannada](https://velvetknot.in/kannada-wedding-traditions/)
- Another Kannada list has 17 ceremonies including Gowri Puja, Jaimala, Jeerige Bella, Akshata and Arundhati Darshan; dhaare = holy water over joined hands, the Kannada form of kanyadaan; present even in Lingayat weddings without a sacred fire — [laddothis Kannada guide](https://coderalert0.github.io/laddothis-site/guides/kannada-hindu-wedding-ceremonies/); [Panigrahana glossary](https://panigrahana.com/kannada-wedding-rituals-glossary.html)
- Kerala: thali tied on a yellow thread at a muhurtham "calculated to the minute"; missing it is "deeply inauspicious"; the day may run 4 am to 10 pm but core rituals (nilavilakku, kanyadaanam, thalikettu) take 60-90 minutes; muhurtham most often 10 am-12:30 pm — [itsmy.wedding, Kerala sequence](https://itsmy.wedding/blog/kerala-wedding-ceremony-sequence)
- Guruvayur temple weddings run daily 5 am-1 pm; photographers focus on pre-dawn preparation at the hotel and outdoor portraits (e.g. Rudra Theertham tank) after the ceremony; pudava (sari the groom gives the bride) is part of the rites — [itsmy.wedding, Guruvayur guide](https://itsmy.wedding/blog/guruvayur-temple-wedding-guide); [Aza Fashions, Kerala ceremonies](https://www.azafashions.com/blog/kerala-wedding-ceremonies-traditions-rituals-2026/); [Guruvayur temple planning guide](https://guruvayurtemple.org/how-to-plan-a-guruvayur-temple-wedding/)
- Telugu albums also feature candid couple portraits "post the ceremony, away from the guests" — [Third Eye Studio](https://thirdeyestudio.mypixieset.com/blog/Telugu-Wedding-in-Dallas/)

### Inferences
- In the south, the thali-tying burst is the single non-negotiable moment; in Kerala it can be a few seconds inside a very short ceremony, so the burst may be small. The app's moment finder should not require a long dense burst to recognise a big moment; a short, very dense burst with the couple and many raised phones/cameras around is typical.
- Temple weddings (Guruvayur) often limit where photographers stand; expect partly blocked faces and crowded frames, and rely more on the post-ceremony portraits for clean couple shots.

### Gaps
- No usable source on Kerala sadya photography, Malayali album conventions, Telugu mangala snanam or Kannada album practice.

## 5. Moments families complain are missing; elders, both sides, vidaai tears

### Takeaway
The most-reported gap is photos with grandparents (they leave early or get missed in rushed group sessions); next, family members left out of group photos, and the groom's pre-wedding functions. Vidaai tears are described as a prized, unrepeatable frame, never a flaw.

## 6. Album sizes, candid vs posed share, details, and how photographers select from thousands

### Takeaway
The standard Indian album is a 12x36-inch "Karizma"-type album sold by sheets (one sheet = one 12x36 spread = two 12x18 pages), typically 30-40 sheets for a multi-event wedding. Photo counts disagree: a dense Indian layout puts ~6 photos per sheet (~240 for 40 sheets), while cleaner layouts use 1-3 per page. Candid is now dominant in metros; one source suggests ~70% candid / 30% traditional. Culling drops soft-focus frames, blinks and duplicate bursts first.

### Cited Findings
- "most Indian print labs print wedding albums on 12x36 inch spreads — two 12x18 pages joined together"; 30-40 sheets (60-80 pages) "lets each event breathe" for multi-event weddings — [Photojaanic, wedding album price in India](https://www.photojaanic.com/blog/wedding-album-price-in-india-with-different-types-of-wedding-album-sheets)
- "40 sheets with about six photos to a sheet make roughly 240 photos"; Album Delhi designs "with an average of 6 photos per sheet, so a 20 sheet album would need 120 photos" — [AccioPix, Karizma album sizes](https://acciopix.com/blog/wedding-album-size); [Album Delhi](https://albumdelhi.com/product/wedding-album-with-brown-printed-leather-bag-12x36/)
- Contrasting sparse layout: 40 pages (20 spreads) holds 50-65 photos, 1-2 per page — [Digital Album Studio](https://digitalalbumstudio.com/guides/how-many-photos-in-a-wedding-album); Zola: 1-3 per page, collage pages 5-10+ — [Zola](https://www.zola.com/expert-advice/how-many-photos-should-be-in-a-wedding-album); Queensberry: 2-3 images per layout on average — [Queensberry](https://queensberry.com/blog/how-many-images-should-i-include-in-my-album)
- 12x36 vs 14x40 are the two common Indian sizes — [Smart Photo Selection](https://smartphotoselection.com/blog/12x36-vs-14x40-wedding-album); [Deluxe Editor, designing 12x36](https://deluxeditor.in/blog/how-to-design-12x36-wedding-album)
- Prices conflict: Album Delhi ₹7,150 for 40 sheets vs a worked example of ₹10,000 for 40 sheets on the same page; IndiaMART listings ₹650 to ₹20,000 per 12x36 album — [AccioPix](https://acciopix.com/blog/wedding-album-size); [IndiaMART](https://www.indiamart.com/proddetail/wedding-album-16245488230.html)
- An older 12x12 Canvera album example: 20 sheets = 40 pages — [Naina.co (2012, author no longer recommends Canvera)](https://www.naina.co/2012/04/exclusive-flushmount-wedding-albums-indian-wedding-photographer)
- Candid/photojournalistic "is the dominant style in metros today"; traditional "ensures rituals, group photos, and important people are covered properly"; most sources recommend a mix; one suggests ~70% candid / 30% traditional (single source) — [HiQ Weddings, styles](https://www.hiqweddings.net/blog/indian-wedding-photography-styles); [FotoOwl, rise of candid](https://fotoowl.ai/blogs/the-rise-of-candid-wedding-photography-in-India-whos-leading-the-way); [Sid Photo](https://sidphoto.in/candid-vs-traditional-wedding-photography/)

### Inferences
- A 30-40 sheet 12x36 album at 2-6 photos per sheet implies roughly 60-240 photos; the app's default of ~15% of the folder (e.g. 300 from 2,000) is on the high side of a printed album. It fits a "shortlist the photographer then lays out" use, which is what the Send screen does. (Inference.)
- Traditional posed family groups survive culling almost regardless of quality because they are the only record of that family; this matches the app's "use their best one" rule.
