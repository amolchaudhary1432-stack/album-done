## Sikh weddings: functions in order, must-have shots, gurdwara photography rules

### Takeaway
A Sikh wedding has pre-wedding functions at home: roka/chunni, maiyan/vatna, jaggo, then chooda and kaleere on the morning. Then come sehrabandi, ghodi, baraat and milni, then the Anand Karaj in the gurdwara (ardas, palla, four lavaan around the Guru Granth Sahib, Anand Sahib, final ardas and hukamnama), then doli and reception. Inside the gurdwara, heads are covered, shoes are off, photography is discreet, flash is often not allowed during ardas and lavaan, and the photographer must never stand in front of the Guru Granth Sahib or make it the subject.

### Cited Findings
**Order and content of functions**
- Maiyan: friends and family take turns rubbing vatna, a paste of turmeric, flour and mustard oil, on the bride. Jaggo ("wake up") marks the start of the festivities, and maiyan may be repeated at the jaggo. — [Punjabi wedding traditions, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjabi_wedding_traditions)
- Chooda: the bride's maternal uncles give the red-and-white bangles, which are dipped in milk first. Golden kalire are tied to them. — [Punjabi wedding traditions, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjabi_wedding_traditions)
- Sources disagree on where chooda falls. One calls it the first ritual of the wedding day; another lists it as a pre-wedding event after maiyan. Sources also disagree on whether kaleere is a step of its own and whether jaggo is the night before or two nights before. — candidates: [weddingz.in guide](https://weddingz.in/real-weddings/a-guide-to-sikh-wedding-rituals-customs-and-traditions), [bollywoodshaadis Sikh bride journey](https://bollywoodshaadis.com/articles/the-journey-of-a-sikh-bride-14878), [indiatribune](https://indiatribune.com/?p=1844) (the snippet did not say which page said which)
- One listed wedding-day order: Choora, Sehra Bandi, Soorma and Kalgi, Ghodi Chadna, Barat, Kaleri, Milni, Anand Karaj, Laavan, Jhota Chupai, Shaggan, Doli. — candidates: [wedbook.in](https://wedbook.in/?p=732), [theweddingfocus](https://www.theweddingfocus.com/?p=4339)
- Milni: the two families meet and exchange garlands. The bride's relatives give shagun to the groom's close relatives in descending order of age. — [Punjabi wedding traditions, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjabi_wedding_traditions)
- The Anand Karaj is the formal Sikh wedding ceremony, held in a gurdwara. — [Punjabi wedding traditions, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjabi_wedding_traditions)
- Doli is the bride's send-off from her parents' home. One source says it happens at the bride's home after the couple's photography is done. — candidates: [bollywoodshaadis](https://bollywoodshaadis.com/articles/the-journey-of-a-sikh-bride-14878), [indianetzone](https://www.indianetzone.com/2/customs_a_sikh_wedding.htm)

**Anand Karaj order of service**
- Ardas: the couple and their parents stand for the opening ardas, which shows the parents' blessing. Everyone else stays seated. — candidates: [Learn Religions program guide](https://www.learnreligions.com/anand-karaj-sikhism-wedding-ceremony-2993016), [SikhiWiki detailed program PDF](https://www.sikhiwiki.org/images/1/1b/Detailed_Wedding_Program.pdf)
- Palla: sources disagree on who does it. One says the groom's sister drapes the palla, a long scarf or turban cloth, over his shoulders. Others say the bride's father places the other end over the bride's shoulder or into her hand, which means she now walks with the groom. — candidates: [SikhNet](https://sikhnet.com/news/anand-karaj-and-lavan), [Learn Religions](https://www.learnreligions.com/anand-karaj-sikhism-wedding-ceremony-2993016), [pataaree](https://pataaree.com/blogs/articles/anand-karaj-ceremony)
- Lavaan: four hymns. The couple walks around the Guru Granth Sahib once per verse, the groom leading and the bride following with the palla. — [Jubily order of service](https://www.jubily.co.uk/guides/anand-karaj-order-of-service); [Laavaan, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laavaan)
- Close: Anand Sahib, a final ardas and the hukamnama (a verse read at random as guidance). — [Jubily order of service](https://www.jubily.co.uk/guides/anand-karaj-order-of-service)

**Gurdwara photography rules**
- Most gurdwaras allow discreet photography but prohibit flash during ardas and lavaan. — [Royal Bindi, photographing in the gurudwara](https://royalbindi.co.uk/things-to-take-care-of-while-taking-photographs-in-the-gurudwara/)
- Photographers cover their heads and take off their shoes inside, and move only where the gurdwara allows. — candidates: [Amatya](https://amatya.co.uk/sikh-wedding-photography/), [Empower Education guide](https://www.empowereducation.co.uk/sikh-wedding-photography-guide.html), [Irvin Sidhu](https://www.irvinsidhu.com/blog/sikh-wedding-traditions-and-how-to-capture-every-sacred-moment-on-camera/)
- Etiquette guides say: sit lower than the Guru Granth Sahib, move slowly, check restrictions with the granthi or family beforehand, and never step in front of the Guru Granth Sahib for a shot. Shots that focus on the scripture itself offend many Sikhs. — same three candidates as above
- Keep your distance from the ceremony while still getting strong shots. — [Royal Bindi Sikh photography tips](https://royalbindi.co.uk/sikh-wedding-photography-tips/)
- Gurdwaras publish their own rules. For example, Sri Guru Singh Sabha Southall has "Anand Karaj Guidelines V1.2 Jan 24", but the snippet did not show its camera clauses. — [SGSSS guidelines PDF](https://sgsss.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/SGSSS-Anand-Karaj-Guidelines-V1.2-Jan-24.pdf)
- Gurdwara interiors, such as stained glass, make good settings for portraits after the ceremony. — [Nick Rose, Southall](https://nick-rose-5mqm.squarespace.com/blog/wedding-photography-at-sri-guru-singh-sabha-gurdwara-in-london)
- Photographers plan around the ceremony rules, shoe and head-covering rules, and the timing of family portraits. — [photosbykishen](https://photosbykishen.co.uk/sikh-wedding-photographer)

### Inferences
- What matters for the album: the palla handover (a father and daughter moment), each of the four lavaan rounds (the same pose four times, so keep one or two, not four), the couple and parents standing at ardas, milni hugs in age order between elders of both sides, chooda with the maternal uncles (mama), and kalire on the bride's hands (a detail shot).
- Gurdwara photos will be flashless and lit by tungsten or window light, taken from the side or behind. Faces are often seen from the side or back during lavaan, and everyone has their head covered (dupatta, patka, rumal). Covered heads change the face crop, so "looking at camera" should not be required in ceremony shots.
- Milni and doli produce many photos of elder men hugging. Strong emotion and tears are normal there.

### Gaps
- No primary page (Sikh Rehat Maryada or a gurdwara rulebook) confirming whether any moment is photo-free. Only "no flash" in some gurdwaras was confirmed.
- Not verified in this session; widely described elsewhere, from background knowledge only:
  - roka/thaka and chunni chadana, where the groom's family places a red chunni on the bride at the engagement
  - kaleere shaken over unmarried girls' heads, the one hit "marries next"
  - gharoli, where the groom's bhabhi fetches water
  - sehrabandi and the sister-in-law applying surma
  - the bride's brothers guiding her round the lavaan
  - karah prasad at the end
  - the Anand Karaj being a morning ceremony
- Reception customs (stage photos with guests) are not specific to Sikh weddings; see the general sections.

## Jain weddings: rituals, Jain differences, album points

### Takeaway
Jain weddings follow the outline of the regional (often Gujarati or Marwari) Hindu wedding, but with Jain prayers: the Navkar mantra, Tirthankaras, and the Mahaveerashtak chanted at the pheras. Steps unique to Jain weddings include mada mandap (purifying the wedding space one or two days before) and a temple visit with donations afterwards. Food is strictly vegetarian, muhurats are by day, and dates avoid Paryushan. Sources disagree on whether the pheras go round a fire or a Tirthankara image, and on whether there are 4 or 7 of them.

### Cited Findings
- Lagna lekhan: a puja at the bride's home where the priest fixes the date and time. Lagna patrika vachan: the muhurat letter is sent to the groom's family, who read it before close relatives after a small puja. — [jainqq.org, Vardhaman Book on Jainism](https://jainqq.org/pagetext/Vardhaman_Book_on_Jainism/006948/118); [culturalindia Jain wedding](https://culturalindia.net/weddings/regional-weddings/jain-wedding.html)
- Khol barana and tikka are pre-wedding rituals, said to be mainly Marwari (for example among Agarwals). — [culturalindia Jain wedding](https://culturalindia.net/weddings/regional-weddings/jain-wedding.html)
- One listing says a Jain wedding runs through 21 ceremonies, including Mada Mandap, Baraat, Kanyavaran and Hasta Melap, and Mangal Pheras. Mada mandap is unique to Jain weddings: it sanctifies the space, usually one or two days before the wedding. — [Jain Wedding Ceremonies in Order (laddothis guide)](https://coderalert0.github.io/laddothis-site/guides/jain-wedding-ceremonies/) (an informal site)
- Wedding day: the groom arrives (ghudchadi, a decorated horse) and is welcomed with aarti. Then come kanyadaan, granthi bandhan (tying the knot) and hastamelap (joining hands). In the pheras the priest chants the Mahaveerashtak Strot while guests sing mangal geet. — [culturalindia](https://culturalindia.net/weddings/regional-weddings/jain-wedding.html); [anthroholic](https://anthroholic.com/jain-marriage); [bollywoodshaadis Jain](https://www.bollywoodshaadis.com/articles/jain-wedding-traditions-and-rituals-5617)
- Pheras: one source says round a sacred fire. Another says round a fire or, in stricter families, an image of a Tirthankara. A third calls the idol version cultural mixing, not a Jain requirement. Most communities take 4 or 7 pheras, each tied to a Jain value (non-violence, truth...). — candidates: [velvetknot Jain traditions](https://velvetknot.in/jain-wedding-traditions/), [vaidikvivah](https://www.vaidikvivah.com/blog/jain-vivah-wedding-rituals), [jainknowledge](https://jainknowledge.com/question/20648/marriage-rituals-in-jain-religion)
- The Navkar (Namokar) mantra is recited to bless the wedding. There is no fixed marriage mantra in the Agamas, so Navkar is used. — candidates: [velvetknot](https://velvetknot.in/jain-wedding-traditions/), [jainknowledge](https://jainknowledge.com/question/20648/marriage-rituals-in-jain-religion)
- Planning rules: strict vegetarian (sattvik) food, daytime muhurats, and dates that avoid Paryushan. In many Digambar communities the father gives the bride's hand. — [velvetknot Jain wedding planner](https://velvetknot.in/weddings/jain-wedding-planner/); [velvetknot Jain traditions](https://velvetknot.in/jain-wedding-traditions/)
- After the wedding: bidaai, then sva graha aagamana (homecoming, with a family prayer at a Jain temple), then jina grahe dhan arpana (donations at a Jain temple), then the reception. — [culturalindia](https://culturalindia.net/weddings/regional-weddings/jain-wedding.html); [laddothis guide](https://coderalert0.github.io/laddothis-site/guides/jain-wedding-ceremonies/)
- Gujarati customs many Gujarati Jains share:
  - In the varghodo, the groom's sister circles a bowl of coins round his head.
  - Gujarati weddings have only four pheras (against seven elsewhere).
  - In hasta melap the priest ties the couple's hands.
  - The bride's sisters steal the groom's shoes. — [Royal Bindi Gujarati traditions](https://royalbindi.co.uk/gujarati-wedding-traditions/); [weddingwire.in Gujarati](https://www.weddingwire.in/wedding-tips/gujarati-wedding--c1225)

### Inferences
- The album should mostly look like a Gujarati or Marwari wedding (mameru, varghodo, mandap, hastamelap). Look for temple visit photos after the wedding as a separate small event.
- Daytime muhurats mean the main ceremony is lit by daylight. The sangeet and reception may be evening events under stage light.
- An idol or image of a Tirthankara may stand in place of the fire. A "fire" cue should not be assumed for the main ritual.

### Gaps
- No good source on Jain-specific photography sensitivities, for example photographing monks or sadhvis who bless the couple, or no photos inside a derasar. Not found.
- Mameru (the maternal uncle's gifts) and chori (the Gujarati four-pillared mandap) were named but not described for Jains.
- Whether a Jain bride or groom leads which pheras: not found.

## Buddhist / Navayana (Ambedkarite) weddings

### Takeaway
Ambedkarite Buddhist weddings, mostly in Maharashtra but also in UP, Punjab, Odisha and Rajasthan, are short and simple. They are officiated by a bhikkhu, Baudhacharya or Dhammachari (a monk is not required), before photos or statues of the Buddha and Dr Ambedkar, sometimes Phule too, with flowers and candles. They include Trisharan and Panchsheel, and often white clothes. There is no fire, saptapadi, havan or kanyadaan. Whether sindoor and mangalsutra are kept is still argued over in the community.

### Cited Findings
- There is no single standard Buddhist wedding ceremony. — [Buddhist wedding, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhist_wedding)
- At a mass wedding of 125 couples in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, the Bhikkhu Sangha recited Trisharan and Panchsheel, and the Buddhist rites came before any Hindu customs. — [Lokmat Times](https://www.lokmattimes.com/aurangabad/125-couples-tie-knot-in-a-unique-ceremony-promoting-harmony-and-cost-saving/amp)
- In Maharashtra, Neo-Buddhist weddings usually have the bride, groom and officiant chant prayers and take oaths before images of the Buddha and Babasaheb Ambedkar. — candidates: [Marathi Buddhists, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marathi_Buddhists), [Scroll](https://amp.scroll.in/article/759598/scroll_in)
- In Punjab, monks chanted Panchsheel and vandana before portraits of Buddha, Phule and Ambedkar. A scholar says Ambedkar's picture was not part of weddings earlier and the trend picked up over the past decade. — [Tribune India](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/punjab/exchanging-vows-amid-chants-skits-with-social-message/696212.html)
- Officiants are Baudhacharyas, Dhammacharis or Bhikkhus, who ask the hosts for photos or statues of Ambedkar and Buddha, flowers, candles and a table. Ambedkar's letter describes a simple ceremony with no havan or saptapadi. Who officiates does not matter as long as the mangal sutta is recited. Bride and groom should wear white. — [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/ground-reports/made-in-heaven-made-india-curious-about-dalit-buddhist-weddings-this-is-how-they-happen/1754900/)
- The ceremony is "simple, dignified, and free of superstition": no kanyadaan, no agni, no rituals of possession or patriarchy. The couple's consent is central, and the couple often lead the planning. — [Plannersy, Maharashtra Buddhist weddings](https://plannersy.com/wedding/maharashtra-buddhist-weddings-meaningful-rituals/)
- There are variants: Maharashtrian Mahar Buddhist and Jatav Buddhist (western UP) Ambedkarite weddings. This comes from a social-media thread, so it is informal. — [threadreaderapp thread](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1691094425966100480.html)
- Ambedkarites in Odisha argue over whether to keep Hindu elements (priests, sindur, mangalsutra). Ambedkarite Buddhist marriage is "an evolving social project". — [Round Table India](https://www.roundtableindia.co.in/selective-ritual-reform-and-buddhist-marriage-among-ambedkarites-in-odisha/)
- Some couples also take vows on the Constitution; for example, Rajasthan and Himachal reports describe vows of equality made before the Constitution or an Ambedkar statue. — [Outlook](https://www.outlookindia.com/culture-society/vows-of-equality-and-constitutional-values-how-a-dalit-couple-in-rajasthan-said-no-to-the-traditional-wedding); [News Arena India](https://www.newsarenaindia.com/states/brothers-in-himachal-take-wedding-vows-before-constitution/60536)
- Buddhist marriages are usually registered under the Hindu Marriage Act or the Special Marriage Act. Courts have sometimes treated them as invalid under the HMA, and that drives the call for a separate law. — [Plannersy](https://plannersy.com/wedding/maharashtra-buddhist-weddings-meaningful-rituals/); [Scroll](https://amp.scroll.in/article/759598/scroll_in)

### Inferences
- The key album shot is the couple (often in white) with folded hands before the Buddha and Ambedkar portraits, with the officiant. Garlands and a blue Panchsheel flag are common in images, but neither was confirmed here. There is no fire or pheras, so the ritual event is short and has few distinct moments.
- Do not apply Hindu rules here: there is no vidaai-as-kanyadaan or saptapadi, and sindoor may be missing.

### Gaps
- No step-by-step order of service, varmala practice or photography rules found. The pre-wedding events (haldi, mehendi, sangeet) practised by many Ambedkarite families are not documented.

## Kashmiri Pandit weddings

### Takeaway
The usual order is kasamdry (engagement), livun (cleaning the house), wanvun (singing by women), maenziraat (mehendi night), devgon (each home separately), then the lagan around the fire with athwas, the dejihor tying and posh puza (flowers showered on the couple under a red cloth, seen as Shiva and Parvati), then vidaai. The bride wears the tarang (headdress) and dejihor (ear ornament) and a pheran. The groom wears a tweed pheran with a sword.

### Cited Findings
- Core sequence: Kasamdry, Livun, Wanvun singing, Maenziraat (mehndi night), Devgun, Lagan with Posh Puza, Kanyadaan and Saptapadi, Vidaai. — candidates: [velvetknot KP rituals](https://velvetknot.in/kashmiri-pandit-wedding-rituals/), [The Memory Trunk KP guide](https://www.thememorytrunk.in/kashmiri-pandit-wedding-guide)
- Livun: house cleaning on an auspicious day. Mud floors used to be redone with fresh mud and water. The two families may hold it on different days. — [weddingwire.in KP](https://www.weddingwire.in/wedding-tips/kashmiri-pandit--c1449); [ikashmir.net marriage](https://ikashmir.net/marriage/index.html)
- Sources disagree on when maenzraat falls: about a week before the wedding (ikashmir), or at the end of the ceremony when the couple sit together (todaystraveller). — [ikashmir.net](https://ikashmir.net/marriage/index.html); [todaystraveller](https://www.todaystraveller.net/?p=73553)
- Devgon/divagon: held separately at each home, with a havan and prayers to Shiva and Parvati. It marks the move from student life to married household life. — candidates: [culturalindia Kashmiri wedding](https://www.culturalindia.net/weddings/regional-weddings/kashmiri-wedding.html), [kashmirica](https://www.kashmirica.com/blog/kashmiri-pandit-wedding/)
- Lagan: the main ceremony, around the sacred fire with a long series of Vedic rituals. Athwas: the couple dip their fingers in a vessel of rice and water. — [plannersy Kashmiri rituals](https://plannersy.com/wedding/kashmiri-wedding-rituals/); [culturalindia](https://www.culturalindia.net/weddings/regional-weddings/kashmiri-wedding.html)
- Posh puza: the closing ritual. The couple sit under a red cloth while guests shower flowers and Vedic mantras are chanted. The couple are seen as Shiva and Parvati. — [weddingaffair](https://weddingaffair.co.in/?p=54286); [weddingsonline](https://www.weddingsonline.in/blog/?p=11401)
- Dejihor (dejhoor): a gold ear ornament tied on during the wedding. It marks a married KP woman, much as the mangalsutra does elsewhere. One source says she goes on wearing it after her husband's death. That seems doubtful and may be a misreading. — [ikashmir.net (Toshkhani)](https://ikashmir.net/sstoshkhani/marriage.html); [culturalindia](https://www.culturalindia.net/weddings/regional-weddings/kashmiri-wedding.html)
- Clothes: the bride wears the tarang headgear, dejhoor, and a pheran (often of raffal) with aari or hook embroidery at the neck, cuffs and edges. The groom wears a tweed pheran with a sword at his waist, and jootis. — [culturalindia Kashmiri wedding](https://www.culturalindia.net/weddings/regional-weddings/kashmiri-wedding.html)
- A real KP wedding writeup describes "10 ceremonies". — [WedMeGood](https://www.wedmegood.com/blog/heartwarming-kashmiri-wedding-with-10-ceremonies-and-a-lot-of-love)

### Inferences
- Must-have album shots: the tarang and dejihor close-ups (detail and identity), posh puza (flowers in the air over the couple under red cloth; motion blur and flower occlusion over faces are expected and are not flaws), wanvun singing (a group of women, candid), and maenziraat with mehendi.
- Many KP weddings now take place outside the Valley (the diaspora after 1990), so venues are hotels and halls. This is background, not verified here.

### Gaps
- Not found: phirsal, satraat, the groom's welcome at the door, or how these appear in albums. Spellings vary a lot between sources (maenzraat, maenziraat, mehendiraat; devgon, divgon, devgun).

## North-East weddings: Assamese, Meitei (Manipuri), Khasi, Naga, Mizo, Bodo

### Takeaway
These are very different from mainland weddings. Assamese weddings are Hindu, women-centred and day-long: juron, tel diya, pani tola, nuoni, daiyan diya, then the fire ceremony. Meitei Luhongba is Vaishnav: kirtan, tulsi, and the bride circling the groom seven times in a potloi to garland him. Khasi weddings are matrilineal, the groom moves in and maternal uncles bless. Naga and Mizo weddings are mostly church white weddings in a single day, with tribal shawls and puans and a big community feast. Bodo weddings use the hathasuni rite, with dokhona, aronai and the Asaari priest. "Family" and "bride's side" work differently here.

### Cited Findings
**Assamese (biya)**
- Juron (juran diya): one or two days before the wedding, the groom's mother and female relatives visit the bride, bringing pan-tamul, gamusa and the trousseau. In tel diya she places a ring and betel nut on the bride's head, pours oil three times and applies sindoor. — candidates: [Pernia's wedding encyclopedia Assam](https://www.perniaspopupshop.com/wedding-encyclopedia/assam), [Sentinel Assam](https://www.sentinelassam.com/north-east-india-news/assam-news/assamese-wedding-a-blend-of-culture-tradition-and-celebration), [nenow on juron](https://nenow.in/?p=347421)
- Pani tola: the mothers fetch water from a pond while women sing biya naam. Nuoni: a ceremonial bath for both bride and groom; mothers apply oil, curd and maah-halodhi (lentil and turmeric paste). — candidates: [utsavpedia Assam](https://www.utsavpedia.com/weddings-festivals/wedding-in-assam/), [assamonline](https://www.assamonline.in/guide/assamese-wedding), [Hotel Vishwaratna](https://vishwaratnahotel.com/assamese-wedding-traditions/)
- Daiyan diya: early on the wedding morning, curd is sent to the bride's house from the groom's. The groom's party "pays a price" to enter. The wedding is held before the sacred fire. — same candidates
- The whole affair takes about 2-3 days. — [culturalindia Assamese wedding](https://culturalindia.net/weddings/regional-weddings/assamese-wedding.html)
- Sources disagree on who gives which gift at juron and on the timing of pani tola and nuoni. One says the bride's brother lifts the groom inside; that is single-source. — same candidates

**Manipuri Meitei (Luhongba)**
- "Lu" means head and "Hongba" means to solemnise. The wedding is accompanied by kirtan, and all ceremonies take place around the tulsi plant. A Hindu Meitei bride wears the potloi; the groom wears a white pheijom, kurta and kokyet. — [India Today NE](https://www.indiatodayne.in/visualstories/webstories/traditions-and-practices-related-to-manipuri-wedding-luhongba-79205-30-11-2023); [eSamskriti](https://esamskriti.com/e/Culture/Ceremonies/Manipuri-Wedding-Rituals-~-Luhongba--1.aspx)
- The bride circles the groom seven times. On the seventh round she puts two garlands on him, and he takes one off and puts it on her. — candidates: [The Imphal Story](https://imphalstory.com/luhongba-punshi-leipun-meitei-wedding/), [velvetknot Manipuri](https://velvetknot.in/manipuri-wedding-traditions/), [Meitei marriage, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meitei_marriage)
- The potloi is a stiff, embroidered cylindrical skirt, also seen in the Ras Lila dance. — same candidates
- Disagreement: Simple Wikipedia says that in the traditional (Sanamahi) Meitei ceremony brides wear phanek and the kajenglei headdress, not the potloi, which belongs to Hindu Meitei practice. — [Meitei wedding, Simple Wikipedia](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meitei_wedding)
- After "Heijing Kharai Puba" the couple are understood to be married. Mangani chakouba, five days later, is the couple's first visit to the bride's home. — [Meitei marriage, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meitei_marriage); [India Today NE](https://www.indiatodayne.in/visualstories/webstories/traditions-and-practices-related-to-manipuri-wedding-luhongba-79205-30-11-2023)

**Khasi (Meghalaya)**
- Society is matrilineal: the husband usually lives with his wife in his mother-in-law's house. — [everyculture Khasi](https://www.everyculture.com/South-Asia/Khasi-Marriage-and-Family.html)
- There are three big life ceremonies: naming, marriage and death. Marriage has a long priest-led ritual and a feast where the maternal uncles of both families bless the couple and formalise the union. — [Ka Jingshai, RKM Shillong](https://kajingshai.rkmshillong.org/postdetails.php?postId=286)
- The bride wears the dhara or jainsem (two contrasting cloths giving a cylindrical shape) and a gold or silver crown with a peak. A priest pours wine on three pieces of fish. There is a libation to the clan's first maternal ancestor, the couple eat from the same plate, and there is a rice cake, then a feast. — candidates: [Ka Jingshai marriage rituals of Meghalaya](https://kajingshai.rkmshillong.org/postdetails.php?postId=57), [saralmarriage Khasi facts](https://blog.saralmarriage.com/9-mindblowing-wedding-facts-khasi-tribe-meghalaya/), [NUJS law review, Khasi customary marriage](https://www.nujs.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Volume-9-Issue-1-1.pdf)
- Many Khasis are Christian. Interfaith weddings exist, for example one officiated jointly by a Unitarian minister and a Khasi-Jaintia traditional religious leader. — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fmeghalaya%2Fbridging-beliefs-historic-interfaith-marriage-in-meghalaya-draws-praise-for-love-and-tolerance-3770409)

**Naga (Nagaland)**
- A Naga wedding is a one-day affair. — [India Today NE, northeast rituals](https://indiatodayne.in/visualstories/webstories/interesting-wedding-rituals-of-northeast-37647-21-05-2023)
- With Christianity (about 150 years), Nagas adopted the white wedding while keeping tribal customs. — [ThePrint, Nagaland white weddings](https://theprint.in/feature/nagaland-loves-grand-white-weddings-it-isnt-looking-to-rest-of-india-for-inspiration/1380113)
- Dress is either fully Western (tuxedo, white dress) or a blend with ethnic wear, the bride in a traditional mekhala. — [ResearchGate, Change and continuity of Naga marriage rituals](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/358959791_CHANGE_AND_CONTINUITY_OF_MARRIAGE_RITUALS_AMONG_THE_NAGA_TRIBES_OF_NAGALAND_A_BRIEF_COMPARATIVE_DISCUSSION)
- Families are expected to feed the whole congregation and village, and the mixed system has become more expensive. — [Morung Express](https://mail.morungexpress.com/westernization-a-threat-to-naga-marriage-system)
- Naga tribes differ. In an Ao engagement the groom offers fish to the bride's parents. Angami ceremonial marriage involves a broker, omens and a bride price. Fowls are sacrificed in traditional rites. — [everyculture Nagas](https://www.everyculture.com/South-Asia/Nagas-Marriage-and-Family.html); [friedeye](https://www.friedeye.com/some-unique-wedding-ritual-and-customs-of-the-north-east-india/)

**Mizo (Mizoram)**
- Most Mizos are Christian, so marriages are solemnised in church. — [Mizoram government, custom of the Mizos](http://mizoram.nic.in/about/custom.htm)
- The bride wears a puan (woven wraparound), now often with a white gown, or the puanchei with a white blouse. The groom wears a (black) suit. The bride gives the groom a puandum (a traditional rug, later used to wrap him at burial). The formal proposal is mangkhawn. — [Pernia's encyclopedia Mizoram](https://www.perniaspopupshop.com/wedding-encyclopedia/mizoram/wedding); [utsavpedia Mizoram](https://www.utsavpedia.com/weddings-festivals/unique-in-every-way-mizoram-wedding/); [Mizo clothing, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mizo_clothing)
- One retailer source says the couple are welcomed with zu (rice beer) at the groom's house. That may conflict with church norms, so treat it as unverified. — [Pernia's encyclopedia Mizoram](https://www.perniaspopupshop.com/wedding-encyclopedia/mizoram)

**Bodo (Boro, Assam)**
- Hathasuni haba is the oldest traditional Bodo marriage, held in the groom's courtyard. The bride carries rice and curry in a wooden hatha and serves the groom and the villagers. It is solemnised by a priest called the Asaari before relatives and the village. — [Bodo-Dimasa archive, Bodo marriage](https://bododimasaarchive.org/digital-heritage/keywords/bodo-marriage); [CIIL Sanchika, Hatha Khurnai book](https://sanchika.ciil.org/items/b5e68d10-aa62-4c19-b4ca-841ee785ec81)
- Clothes: the groom wears gamsa, aronai and gosla. At this rite the bride wears a white dokhona (white means purity), and a red thaosi dokhona on the wedding day. The dokhona is the identity dress of Boro women. — [r/assam comment](https://nyc1.lr.ggtyler.dev/r/assam/comments/1hbopwh/hathasuni_haba_हथसन/m1kyyah/?context=3) (informal); [Bodo-Dimasa archive](https://bododimasaarchive.org/digital-heritage/keywords/bodo-marriage)
- Entry rite: the bride stands on a gambari khamplai (a stool), and the groom's mother or sister washes her feet with holy water before she enters. Hinjau gwdan is a pre-wedding rite. — [Bodo-Dimasa archive, Hinjau gwdan](https://bododimasaarchive.org/digital-heritage/%e0%a4%b9%e0%a4%bf%e0%a4%a8%e0%a4%9c%e0%a4%be%e0%a4%b5-%e0%a4%97%e0%a5%8b%e0%a4%a6%e0%a4%be%e0%a4%a8-%e0%a4%86%e0%a4%a5%e0%a4%bf-%e0%a4%b8%e0%a5%81%e0%a4%a8%e0%a4%be%e0%a4%af-hinjau-gwdan)

### Inferences
- In church weddings (Naga, Mizo, many Khasi), the album follows the Western church order: entry, vows, rings, kiss or blessing, signing, the couple outside the church, then the feast. Church interiors may restrict where the photographer can stand, though no source confirmed this.
- The Meitei seven-circle garlanding is a sequence like the pheras, so keep one or two frames. The bride's potloi is a strong detail and full-length shot.
- In Khasi weddings the maternal uncles take the role of elders. Bride's side and groom's side labels still apply.
- Assamese functions are women-centred (mothers, aunts, the groom's mother at juron). The groom's mother appears at the bride's function, so the app's side guess from "who appears at the bride's pre-wedding events" can be wrong for Assamese weddings.

### Gaps
- No photography rules or album writeups by NE photographers found. Little on Khasi Christian church order, Naga reception detail, or Bodo wedding-day order (khurnai). "Ka lympei" returned nothing.
- Background knowledge, not verified here: Assamese "aathmangala" (visiting the bride's home on the eighth day) appears in the title of one source ([Hotel Vishwaratna](https://vishwaratnahotel.com/assamese-wedding-traditions/)) but its content was not seen.

## Album formats and numbers in India: sheets, photos per sheet, total photos, candid share

### Takeaway
Indian albums are sold by the sheet (one sheet = two pages, usually 12x36 inch panoramic spreads, Karizma or Canvera style). For multi-event weddings the common range is 30-40 sheets, about 60-80 pages. Densities run from about 6 photos a sheet down to 2-3 a spread in modern "magazine" albums, so the final album is roughly 80-300 photos. The couple is often asked to shortlist 150-500 from a delivery of 2,000-5,000. The commonly quoted candid to posed split is 70:30. All figures are vendor rules of thumb.

### Cited Findings
- One sheet is two pages, front and back. Multi-event weddings need 30-40 sheets (60-80 pages) so each event has room. The printer charges per sheet and the photographer designs per sheet. — candidates: [Photojaanic album price India 2026](https://www.photojaanic.com/blog/wedding-album-price-in-india-with-different-types-of-wedding-album-sheets), [Acciopix Karizma album sizes and sheets](https://acciopix.com/blog/wedding-album-size), [Memoriffy photo book India](https://memoriffy.com/guide/wedding-photo-book-india)

## The culling and selection process and what makes a photo "album-worthy" in India

### Takeaway
Photographers cull in passes: first reject blinks, blur, missed focus and misfires; then group bursts and duplicates and keep the best frame of each moment by expression, composition and light; then check the sequence. Couples are then asked to shortlist from the edited set, event by event, in time order, and the photographer or lab designs the album with soft-copy proofs and limited revision rounds. Indian guides call album-worthy: emotion (vidaai tears, elders' blessings, parents' reactions), candid couple moments (glances, laughter), the key rituals, varmala, bridal portraits, and family groups, which they class as essential even though they are staged.

### Cited Findings
- Multi-pass culling: a "blink check" first (blur, blinks, misfires), then group duplicates and keep the best frame from each moment, then a final look at the sequence. — candidates: [Imagen culling](https://imagen-ai.com/valuable-tips/what-is-culling-in-photography/), [Narrative culling FAQ](https://narrative.so/blog/photo-culling-complete-faq), [Timeless PV culling](https://timelesspv.com/what-is-culling-the-essential-photo-selection-process/)
- Reject blinks, missed focus and redundant frames before editing. AI tools flag closed eyes, soft focus and near-duplicate bursts, but are only a starting point and need review. — [Imagen editing best practices](https://imagen-ai.com/valuable-tips/wedding-photo-editing-best-practices-for-photographers/); [Narrative FAQ](https://narrative.so/blog/photo-culling-complete-faq)
- Among near-identical shots, pick the best frame by composition, light and expression. Use detail shots (rings, flowers, décor) as transitions between parts of the day. Avoid faces in the gutter (the album fold) and mixed colour grading. — candidates: [Mixtiles](https://mixtiles.com/blog/photo-books/choose-photos-wedding-album), [Sortra](https://sortra.com/how-to-choose-wedding-photos-for-album/), [Imagen album design](https://imagen-ai.com/valuable-tips/design-wedding-album/)
- Remove duplicates, poor focus and similar-looking group portraits. Arrange the album pre-wedding, wedding, reception, in time order. — candidates: [Folio Albums](https://www.folioalbums.com/how-to-choose-photos-for-the-perfect-wedding-album/), [pikperfect](https://www.pikperfect.com/blog/designing-a-beautiful-indian-wedding-album/)
- Choose images that represent each event (baraat, ceremony) with a few portraits and candids from each. Start from the 12-15 most expressive images. — candidates: [pikperfect](https://www.pikperfect.com/blog/designing-a-beautiful-indian-wedding-album/), [samaro.ai](https://www.samaro.ai/blogs/crafting-the-perfect-indian-wedding-album)
- Timeline: most photographers take 4-8 weeks to edit a full Indian wedding, so the couple selects after editing. Candid editing takes more work because each frame is edited on its own. — [Memoriffy](https://memoriffy.com/guide/wedding-photo-book-india) (likely); [shareurwedding candid guide](https://shareurwedding.com/candid-wedding-photography/)
- Revisions: some designers offer unlimited free revisions, others two rounds. The maker shows a soft-copy preview for corrections and approval. — candidates: [Photojaanic](https://www.photojaanic.com/photo-books/wedding-photo-album), [ultraaalbums Mumbai](https://ultraaalbums.com/), [Prolab 2026 guide](https://www.prolab.in/blog/how-to-create-the-perfect-wedding-photo-album-complete-guide/)
- Some Indian studios cull and select themselves ("no need to pore over contact sheets"). — [photobrahma31 pricing FAQ](https://photobrahma31.mypixieset.com/investment/)
- Vidaai: a tear-filled moment that "must be beautifully captured". The tears belong in the album, not only the happy moments. — candidates: [thetamarindtree must-have shots](https://www.thetamarindtree.in/blog/wedding-shots-album), [venuelook 15 must-have shots](https://www.venuelook.com/blog/15-must-have-shots-in-your-wedding-album), [bollywoodshaadis 10 touching moments](https://bollywoodshaadis.com/amp-articles/10-beautiful-and-touching-moments-every-indian-wedding-album-must-have-3443)
- Elders: emotional elders, siblings cheering up elders, and heartfelt blessings from elders are classic candid moments. — candidates: [WedMeGood candid portraits](https://www.wedmegood.com/blog/wmgmusthaves-gleaming-candid-portraits-in-your-wedding-album), [WeddingSutra 30 emotional family moments](https://www.weddingsutra.com/blog/30-emotional-wedding-moments-that-celebrate-family-bonds/)
- Also listed: a candid varmala amid cheers and laughter, the couple sharing a smile, a whisper or a stolen glance, the bridal portrait, and décor details. — same candidates plus [bollywoodshaadis must-have bride photos](https://www.bollywoodshaadis.com/articles/top-10-photos-every-indian-bride-must-have-in-her-wedding-album-3531)
- Parents' emotional expressions, grandparents watching the ceremony, and relatives meeting after years become treasured memories. — candidates: [WeddingWire India family photos](https://www.weddingwire.in/wedding-tips/indian-family-photos--c9487), [studiomemorylane](https://www.studiomemorylane.com/10-must-have-wedding-photos-every-indian-couple-should-capture/)

### Inferences
- The app's order of steps (quality gate, keep the best of each burst, one per pose, story in time order, details as transitions) matches how photographers work. One thing to add: when couples shortlist, they go event by event. The app's per-event view and "what we left out" sheet should keep that grouping.
- "Faces in the gutter" is a layout problem the app does not have (it does not design pages), but it is worth knowing if the app ever suggests page layouts.
- Emotion, not technical perfection, makes a photo album-worthy in Indian photographers' words. Tears, elders' expressions and reactions should get a bonus, not a penalty. The app's "tears are never a flaw" rule matches this.

### Gaps

## Family group photos, reception stage photos, and complaints about missing people

### Takeaway
Family groups are a core, non-negotiable part of an Indian album: three generations, grandparents on both sides, parents and in-laws. They are the most often underestimated and most often missed shots, because relatives wander off and the ceremonies run late. Photographers ask for a family list ahead of time and a family coordinator, and budget about 2-3 minutes per group, often moving the extended family to the reception. The most common regret is "no photo of me with my side of the family".

### Cited Findings
- A group photo, though staged, is a must-have. Take small groups with parents and in-laws after the wedding. — [Shan Photography checklist](https://www.shanphotography.com/blog/must-have-indian-wedding-photos-checklist/)
- People always underestimate how long family photos take at Indian weddings, and relatives drift off to catch up with cousins. Share a shot list for family groups in advance, and call family in batches. — [All About Eve, wedding photography mistakes](https://allabouteve.co.in/wedding-photography-mistakes-to-avoid-wedding-planning-tips-wedding-advice-2026/); [WeddingWire India family photos](https://www.weddingwire.in/wedding-tips/indian-family-photos--c9487) (snippet did not say which)
- Give the photographer a family group list naming who is in each group, with priorities, and appoint a family coordinator to round up relatives. — [Hitkari Productions 2026 guide](https://hitkariproductions.com/indian-wedding-photography-guide-2026/)
- About 2-3 minutes per group shot. One sample timeline puts close-family formals at the start of the reception. — [Michael Santos, Hindu wedding timeline](https://www.michaelsantosphotography.com/ideal-hindu-wedding-timeline-for-photography/)
- Big extended-family groups (cousins, aunts, uncles) are often moved to the reception. — [Cara Eliz family shot list](https://caraelizphoto.com/resources/a-complete-wedding-day-family-photo-shot-list/) (US source)
- Commonly listed family shots: three-generation portraits (bride, mother, grandmother; groom, father, grandfather) and grandparents from each side. — [WedMeGood photo list](https://www.wedmegood.com/blog/building-a-photo-list-must-have-photos-you-dont-want-to-miss/)
- A bride may not want to look at vidaai photos again, but will want pictures with her parents and siblings. — [bollywoodshaadis must-have bride photos](https://www.bollywoodshaadis.com/articles/top-10-photos-every-indian-bride-must-have-in-her-wedding-album-3531)
- Real complaint: a parent had given the photographer a list, yet there were no photos of her daughter and her side of the family. The bride felt guilty afterwards. — [Mumsnet thread](https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/am_i_being_unreasonable/4323721-To-be-feeling-guilty-about-key-missing-family-wedding-photos) (UK)
- Reception checklists: décor, the grand entrance, speeches. — [Manoj Lohar shot list](https://manojlohar.com/essential-shot-list-indian-wedding-photography/)
- Studios build the family shot list with the couple's families as part of the package. — [Surya Garigipati packages](https://www.suryagarigipati.com/journal/indian-wedding-photography-packages-explained)
- Most couples get guest photos from only 5-10 close relatives and never see the rest (a sharing-service claim). — [Memorias wedding photo sharing](https://www.memorias.in/memories/wedding-photo-sharing)
- Old archive albums show sheets made of portraits of the couple standing with guests, i.e. reception stage photos with guests are a long-standing album format. — [British Library EAP946 album sheet](https://eap.bl.uk/index.php/item/EAP946-27-1-1-363)

### Inferences
- The stage queue at the reception, where every family comes up for a photo with the couple, makes dozens to hundreds of near-identical frames. Families care that their group is in the album. This supports the app's rule: one best frame per family group, but every distinct group kept, plus a warning for anyone with only poor photos.
- The most painful miss is a whole side, usually the bride's maternal side or the grandparents. A side-balance check and "elders first" are the right guards.
- For Assamese, Khasi and Bodo weddings, which elders count as key differs (mothers at juron; maternal uncles in Khasi weddings). Do not hard-code "father's side" seniority.

### Gaps
- No Indian survey of album complaints was found; the evidence is forum posts and vendor advice. No source gave the share of the album that family groups take (for example "a third of the sheets"). This would be worth measuring from real albums (e.g. wedding-1's PDF).
